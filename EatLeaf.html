Eat Leaf


Description

Eat Leaf is a relaxing game where you play as a cute caterpillar eating leaves while avoiding spikes and pitfalls. Explore a dark environment with only a small flashlight to light your path.

Project Info

Role: Solo Developer
Engine: Godot
Date: February-March 2024
Notes: Taught myself Godot and GDScript to create this
game.
Play in browser

Personal Notes

  • This was my first game ever and my first experience in a game engine!!!
  • I felt like I wasn't learning enough through my classes so I decided to teach myself how to use Godot.
  • I studied the documentation and watched tutorials to learn GdScript, learned to navigate the engine, and taught myself how to do pixel art, creating this 2d platformer in 2 months.
  • It was very time/energy consuming and it was difficult to not burn out. I learned the importance of breaks. Coming back to look at a bug or problem with fresh eyes often helped me solve my problems faster than if I stared at the same lines for hours.
