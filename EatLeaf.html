Eat Leaf


Description

Eat Leaf is a relaxing game where you play as a cute caterpillar eating leaves while avoiding spikes and pitfalls. Explore a dark environment with only a small flashlight to light your path.

Project Info

Role: Solo Developer
Engine: Godot
